About Me

Get to know the artist behind the beautiful designs

Hello, I'm Zara!

A passionate mehndi artist with a love for creating intricate, meaningful designs.

I started my journey 8 years ago when I discovered my passion for henna art during my sister's wedding. What began as a hobby quickly turned into a fulfilling career.

Over the years, I've had the honor of working with hundreds of brides, creating designs that range from traditional Indian patterns to modern Arabic styles.
